Dear GenBank/RefSeq staff,

Two things, the first of which is a correction to our own record rather than to yours.

1. A MISSTATEMENT IN OUR MESSAGE OF 2026-10-23

In section 4 of that message, about GCF_002290505.1, we described the adjacent accession
GCF_002290495.1 as one "we reported to you separately". That is not so, and we apologise for it.
Our earlier report of 2026-10-22 deliberately set both members of that pair aside, because at the
time the only evidence we had for either was negative: ST58 was absent from the S. flexneri
assemblies we had typed, which is not the same as its being attested among S. sonnei. No separate
report was ever sent. Nothing else in the 2026-10-23 message depends on that sentence, and the
evidence given there for GCF_002290505.1 stands as written.

2. GCF_002290495.1, reported here for the first time

  GCF_002290495.1  "Shigella flexneri"  ->  Shigella sonnei

      MLST (ecoli_achtman_4) ST58. Six of its seven alleles are identical to the S. sonnei
      assembly GCF_013374815.1 (ST155), differing only at gyrB. The S. flexneri controls we typed
      are ST245 and ST1022 and share no allele with it at adk, fumC, mdh, purA or recA.
      Mash distance (k=21, s=10000) 0.000074 to GCF_002248355.1, which is deposited as S. sonnei
      and is also ST58, against 0.005480 to its nearest S. flexneri: a 74-fold contrast.

      What has changed since 2026-10-22 is that the evidence is now positive rather than absent.
      ST58 is attested among your S. sonnei records: GCF_002248345.1, deposited as S. sonnei by
      the same submitter on 2017-08-11, carries all seven ST58 alleles
      (adk-6 fumC-4 gyrB-4 icd-16 mdh-24 purA-8 recA-14). So ST58 is not merely missing from
      S. flexneri; it is present in S. sonnei.

      GCF_002290495.1 and GCF_002290505.1 are 0.000036 apart and are the same organism. Neither is
      S. flexneri. GCF_002290505.1 was reported in our 2026-10-23 message; this completes the pair.
